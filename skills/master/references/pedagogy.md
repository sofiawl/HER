# Pedagogy references for master

These are the methods the skill encodes. Primary sources are textbooks and review articles; no need to web-search each session.

## Project-based learning (PBL)

Learning goal rides on a real artifact. Milestones match deliverables; gates ensure she can explain the artifact, not only submit it.

## Cognitive load (Sweller)

**Intrinsic load:** domain and architecture she must hold (layers, invariants, failure modes).

**Extraneous load:** boilerplate, config noise, repetitive CRUD she will not be examined on.

Master removes extraneous via `may_implement`; gates protect intrinsic via `must_explain`.

## Retrieval practice (testing effect)

Short checks at teach gates and the defense exam beat re-reading slides. Failed retrieval gets a correction and a later retry in the exam sample.

## Feynman / elaboration

Her one-line summary after each topic forces compression; your checks probe whether the line is memorized jargon or usable understanding.

## Zone of proximal development

Early milestones: more `may_implement`, narrow `must_explain`. Later milestones: shift topics toward must_explain as the codebase exists to anchor on.

Reclassify rule prevents shifting topics down without rubric-backed approval.

## What this skill is not

Not spaced-repetition software (no Anki deck unless she asks). Not a substitute for attending class. Not academic integrity bypass: she must be able to defend the submitted work.
